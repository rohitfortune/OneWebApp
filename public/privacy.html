Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Local-First Architecture

One is a local-first application. All of your notes, passwords, credit card information, and files are encrypted and stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. We do not operate any backend servers, and we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data.

2. Cloud Storage Integration

If you choose to use the Cloud Backup features (such as Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive integration), the application communicates directly with those third-party APIs from your browser. Your encrypted vault data is synced directly to your personal cloud storage account. We never have access to your data, your encryption keys, or your cloud storage credentials.

3. Authentication Data

When you sign in with Google or Microsoft to access your cloud storage, the OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device. We do not track your logins or share your authentication state with anyone.
